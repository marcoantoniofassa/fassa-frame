# Object To Stone prompts

Replace bracketed details with the actual subject and scene. Preserve the identity, framing, motion and lighting constraints. Request a cost estimate and obtain authorization before generation. Model settings, observed limitations and assembly steps are in [README.md](README.md).

```
Replace the [white pillow] the man is holding and throwing with a large, heavy, realistic grey granite boulder rock of the same size, rough stone texture, natural cracks. The rock follows exactly the same path, position, rotation and timing as the [pillow] in every frame: [lifted above his head, thrown forward and landing on the carpet in front of him], where it stays as a solid rock. Keep everything else exactly the same: the man's face, [glasses, beard, hair, white polo shirt, jeans, shoes], hands, all body motion, the room, [carpet], lighting, framing and camera.
```

Warm soft indoor lighting from above, realistic contact shadows, shallow depth of field, shot on a 35mm lens, high detail, no people, no other text.
```

Keep the letters facing the camera and readable in one line; exclude all other text. Match exact user wording and its language rather than translating supplied speech.
